# Buyer Language

> **Why this file exists:** the video's first proof check is "demand language," meaning real phrases buyers use. Without this file, the Scout can only invent *plausible* phrases. With it, every line in the offer can be traced to something a real person said.

**Rules:**
- **Paste, don't paraphrase.** Typos and all.
- **Anonymize.** Use "Client A," never a name. This project is public.
- **Raw transcripts with names** go in `data/private/` instead.

---

## Quick-start: ask 5 people these 2 questions

Send by DM or email to 5 clients, past clients, or subscribers:

1. "In your own words, what limits your income right now?"
2. "What have you tried with AI so far, and how did it go?"

Paste their answers below.
