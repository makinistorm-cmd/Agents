# Channel Analytics

> **Why this file exists:** searchability was the weakest score (2/5) because the brief has no data on what your audience searches for or clicks. Your own channel already has that answer.

**Where to find it:** YouTube Studio → **Analytics** → **Content** (top videos) and → **Research** / **Traffic source: YouTube search** (search terms). Use the last 90 days, or "Lifetime" if your channel is new.

You can also export a spreadsheet (the download arrow in Analytics) and save it in `data/private/`. The agents can read it there.
