# Relay Test Log

> **Why this file exists:** the video only works if the Relay actually beats a strong single prompt. Nothing in the brief can predict that. Only a real test can. This log is your proof, and its results fill the `[brackets]` in Emails 3 and 4 and in the CTA.

**Rules (from the go/no-go test):**
1. Run the strong control prompt **3 times**. Keep the best output. Don't pick a weak one.
2. Run the Relay **once**, unedited.
3. Score each check: **Yes** (present, with the exact line that proves it) or **No**.
4. **Go = 2 or 3 Yes.** 0–1 = don't film this idea. Tighten the brief, add real buyer language, and retest.
5. **Never edit either output** to make the gap look bigger.

**The strong control prompt** (from the video plan; swap in your test business details):
> "I'm a [coach / consultant / ...] doing [free discovery calls]. [Referrals keep me busy] but everything I sell is 1:1 and priced by the hour. Help me productize this into a fixed-price offer with tiers, a name, and pricing that isn't tied to my time. Make it sound premium and specific to my niche, not generic."
