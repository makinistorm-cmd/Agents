## Offer Architecture

### Positioning

**One sharp positioning statement:**
"Everyone's teaching Claude Code to developers. FuturIQ teaches it to build your business."

**Offer formula statement (for on-camera use):**
"I help coaches, consultants, and creators stuck tool-hopping with AI achieve a specific, priced, sellable offer in under 15 minutes — without hiring a strategist, learning to code, or guessing at their positioning — by building a 4-agent Claude Code system (**The Revenue Relay**) that turns one business idea into positioning, an offer, content angles, and a lead-gen sequence."

Deliberate word choice: never say "agents," "orchestration," or "subagent" on camera. Say "teammates," "your strategist," "your researcher," "your closer."

### Core Offer

*The Revenue Systems Build* — a paid, done-with-you engagement where FuturIQ configures a personalized "Revenue Relay" agent team for the buyer's actual business and hands them the finished positioning + offer + content + lead-gen package, plus the reusable system. The video's job is to make one specific, priced offer appear on screen as proof — that proof point *is* the ad for the Revenue Systems Build.

### Unique Mechanism — "The Revenue Relay"

Four AI teammates hand off a baton until a raw business idea becomes a sellable offer:

1. **The Scout** (market-signal research) — checks if anyone's actually hungry for this: where the crowd is, what they fear, what they secretly wish were true. *Market validation in minutes, not a $5K research retainer.*
2. **The Strategist** (offer architecture) — turns research into one sentence you could sell today: who, what pain, what result, why believable. *Replaces "what do I even sell?" paralysis.*
3. **The Publisher** (content angles) — turns one offer into content angles that all point at the same sale. *Content with a commercial spine.*
4. **The Closer** (lead magnet + follow-up) — builds the free thing that gets the email and the sequence that turns that email into a buyer. *The video fills a pipeline.*

Why it's believable: shown live, on a real business idea, producing a real priced offer in real time.

### Demo Business Idea Recommendation

Feed the system: *"A 1:1 business coach who helps other coaches and consultants land clients — currently doing everything through free discovery calls and referrals, wants a scalable offer instead of trading more hours."*

Relatable to the exact audience; plausibly produces: *"I help business coaches book high-ticket clients without cold outreach or more sales calls by building a Lead Qualification & Booking Agent that pre-sells prospects before they ever get on the phone."* Avoid SaaS/e-commerce examples — they reintroduce the "this is for developers" problem.

### Proof Requirements

1. The actual offer sentence generated live, unedited.
2. A visible before/after: vague idea at minute 1 → specific priced offer by minute 10–12.
3. Plain-English lower-thirds every time a teammate activates.
4. A visible timer proving the sub-15-minute promise.
5. Generated lead magnet and follow-up sequence briefly shown.
6. A visible price on the final offer.
7. An honesty moment: "this doesn't replace customer interviews — it compresses the first draft of that work."
