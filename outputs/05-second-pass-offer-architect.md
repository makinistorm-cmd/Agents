## Rewrites

**Audience:** The 1:1 expert who is booked, not broke. Coach, consultant, or freelance specialist who already gets referrals and discovery calls but sells only their time. Creators and productized services are secondary.

**Core pain:** Income is rented, not owned. It exists only while they're on the call. Demand goes up, hours go up, income doesn't compound.

**Positioning:** "Your calendar is not a business model."

**Promise:** Not "a priced offer" (a means ChatGPT already fakes). An offer that isn't sold by the hour, plus the content and follow-up that sell it, all pointed at one sale.

**Mechanism insight:** "You validate before you price, and you price before you sell. Do it in one pass and you're just decorating a guess." When one pass researches and writes, the writer bends the research to fit the sentence it wants. Separate passes mean the Strategist can only use what the Scout actually found.

**Proof additions:** Demo idea in the buyer's real words. NEW control beat: plain prompt + generic output shown before the Scout runs; Strategist output later split-screened against it. Closer output framed as "what sells it while you're not on a call."

**Structure:** Proposed cutting Publisher (3 roles) to afford the control beat; 14:00 = hook 0:20, thesis 1:10, team 0:30, control 0:35, Scout 3:25, Strategist 3:35, honesty 0:20, Closer montage 1:55, ladder 0:45, recap 0:45, CTA 0:40.
