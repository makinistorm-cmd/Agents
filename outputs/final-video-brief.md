## 2. The Bet

Everyone is teaching AI agent teams to developers. Nobody is showing a booked-out expert how to use one to stop selling hours.

That's the video.

---

## 3. Who It's For

**The 1:1 expert who is booked, not broke.**

A coach, consultant, or freelance specialist. Referrals keep them full. Discovery calls fill the week. Demand is proven, but everything they sell is their time. A better month means more hours, not more revenue per hour.

They know they need "an offer." It never gets built, because building one means hiring a strategist or finding weeks they don't have.

*If it doesn't describe someone who is already busy and still not scaling, it isn't this person.* Creators and productized service businesses can buy further up the ladder. They are not who the hook is for.

> Coordinator note: this deliberately narrows the brief's original audience list. A video that speaks to one buyer converts that buyer. A video that speaks to four converts none.

---

## 4. The Pain

**Their income is rented, not owned.**

It only exists while they're on the call. Demand rises, hours rise, income doesn't compound. When they take a week off or want to raise their rate, the referral pipeline doesn't care.

Every discovery call is a scoping conversation that could have been a sale.

---

## 5. Positioning and Promise

**Positioning line:**
> Your calendar is not a business model.

**Offer statement:**
> I help booked-out coaches and consultants turn their expertise into an offer that isn't priced by the hour, without hiring a strategist or writing sales copy, by running The Revenue Relay: four AI teammates that validate the idea, price it, and build the assets that sell it.

**The promise (and what it isn't):**
It isn't "you'll get an offer sentence." A single ChatGPT prompt can fake that.

It is **an offer that isn't sold by the hour, and the content and follow-up that pre-sell it before the call**, all pointed at one sale.

---

## 6. The Mechanism — The Revenue Relay

| Teammate | Job |
|---|---|
| **Scout** | Tests whether there's real demand, before anyone writes a word |
| **Strategist** | Turns validated demand into one priced, sellable offer |
| **Publisher** | Turns that offer into content that points at the sale |
| **Closer** | Builds the lead magnet and follow-up that turn attention into buyers |

**The insight:**
> Validate before you price. Price before you sell. Do it all in one pass and you're decorating a guess.

**Why it isn't four renamed prompts:** when one pass researches the market *and* writes the pitch, it bends the research to fit the sentence it already wants to write. Hope gets mistaken for evidence. Separate passes mean the Strategist can only use what the Scout actually found.

The video doesn't just say this. It shows it with the control beat.

## 13. Cut List

- ❌ **Tool tours, feature walkthroughs, settings screens.**
- ❌ **Developer vocabulary on camera:** agent, subagent, orchestration, prompt chain, YAML.
- ❌ **"Save time," "10x," "productivity."** The promise is an offer, not hours back.

- ❌ **"AI is changing everything" openers.** Open on their calendar.
- ❌ **A premium-service pitch in the video.** One ask. Email carries the rest.
- ❌ **Calling it a "14-minute build."** The timer says what the build took.
- ❌ **Made-up proof.** No invented testimonials, stats, or results. Every example gets run for real before it appears in copy.
