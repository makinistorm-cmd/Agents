## 5. The Unique Mechanism — The Revenue Relay

Four AI teammates pass a baton. Each one has **one job** and hands its work to the next. That hand-off is the system, and the system is what you're really selling.

| # | Teammate | Job (plain English) | Business translation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scout** | Checks whether anyone is actually hungry for this: what they want, fear, and secretly wish were true | Market validation in minutes instead of a research retainer |
| 2 | **Strategist** | Turns the research into one sentence you could sell today: who, what pain, what result, why it's believable, what it costs | Ends "what do I even sell?" paralysis |
| 3 | **Publisher** | Turns that offer into content angles that all point at the same sale | Content with a commercial spine |
| 4 | **Closer** | Builds the free download that gets the email, and the sequence that turns the email into a buyer | The video fills a pipeline instead of just getting views |

**Why this beats "just prompting ChatGPT":** generic AI output comes from skipping research. The Relay forces Scout to run before Strategist writes a word. *Skip Scout, get generic. Run Scout, get specific.*

**On-camera vocabulary rule:** never say "agent," "subagent," or "orchestration." Say "teammate," "your Scout," "your Strategist."

**Why they want it now:** the loudest question when the video ends is *"will this work on mine?"* The template answers it in 20 minutes with the same teammates. It's not a PDF to read. It's the system to run.

## 10. What the Creator Must Show on Screen (Credibility)

1. **The demo idea typed live** at 1:30. A vague one-liner, visibly rough.
2. **Each teammate's real output** appearing on screen, not a pasted-in result. Speed up the waiting, never fake the output.
3. **One visible revision** by the Strategist, so it reads as a build, not a magic trick.
4. **The priced offer sentence, full screen, held in silence.** A number makes it feel sellable instead of theoretical.
5. **The before/after split screen** of the idea you typed next to the offer it built.
6. **A real-time build timer** that starts at the Scout, stops at the Closer, and is shown frozen at the end.
7. **Plain-English lower-thirds** every time a teammate activates.
8. **The Publisher and Closer outputs**, briefly but for real. That's what backs up the "full system" claim.
9. **The honesty beat** on camera: "This compresses the first draft. It doesn't replace customer conversations."
10. **The template itself** at the CTA: open the folder so viewers see exactly what they're downloading.

## 11. What to Remove Because It Feels Generic

- ❌ **Any tool tour.** No "here are 5 AI tools," no feature walkthroughs, no settings screens.
- ❌ **Developer vocabulary:** agent, subagent, orchestration, system prompt, YAML, frontmatter. If the terminal shows it, cover it with a lower-third or zoom past it.
- ❌ **"AI will save you time"** or "10x your productivity" lines. The promise is a sellable offer, not saved hours.
- ❌ **Income claims:** "$5K offer," "$10K/month," "fill your calendar." (The agent's "Your $5K Offer" thumbnail was cut.)
- ❌ **Prompt-engineering tips.** The brief explicitly rules this out.
- ❌ **Equal time for all four teammates.** Scout and Strategist earn the screen time. Publisher and Closer are proof, not tutorials.
- ❌ **AI hype openers** ("AI is changing everything…"). Start with the viewer's problem.
- ❌ **A spoken pitch for the premium service.** One ask in the video (the template). Emails handle the rest.
- ❌ **"14-minute build" claims.** The video is 14 minutes. The build is whatever the timer says.
- ❌ **Niche-only framing.** The coach is the *example*, not the audience. Keep saying "your idea."
