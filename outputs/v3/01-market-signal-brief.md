## Market Signal Brief — "Stop Selling Hours" (narrowed: booked 1:1 expert)

### Demand Pattern
**Evidence:** "Stop trading hours for dollars" is an established, monetized category — a dedicated paid roadmap product (stoptradinghoursfordollars.com), a Forbes Coaches Council piece ("How To Stop Trading Hours For Dollars As A Busy Entrepreneur," 2024), an existing YouTube video aimed at this buyer ("Coaches: Stop Trading Time for Money (Do This Instead)"), and a wider productization content industry (consultingsuccess.com, usetabby.com, multiple 2025/2026 posts on the "1:1 income ceiling").

**Thinner:** No evidence of a YouTube-native "one plain prompt vs. multi-agent workflow" genre. "ChatGPT vs. custom AI agent" exists mainly as B2B blog/comparison pages (2026). *Inference:* the control beat is plausible but unproven as a video mechanic.

### Audience Psychology
- **Wants:** proof AI can do strategist-level thinking without hiring anyone or learning to code.
- **Fears:** another "AI will 10x you" tool tour; a rigged generic-vs-custom comparison.
- **Misunderstands:** conflates "I need more leads" with "I need an offer."
- **Secretly hopes:** the fix is a system run once, not a new skill or a hire.
- **Sophistication: intermediate-to-skeptical.** Daily ChatGPT users — which *raises* the bar for the control beat.

### Risk
**Biggest:** the control prompt might not be bad enough. Modern single-shot output for "write me an offer for a coach" can already sound specific. If it isn't visibly generic, the gap argument collapses into a strawman this ChatGPT-fluent audience will call out.
**Secondary:** saturated advice space — differentiation rides entirely on the demo being real and the gap being wide.

### Strategic Recommendation
1. **Proceed.** Narrowing and the pain reframe are supported by market evidence.
2. **No strawman.** Run the control prompt multiple times before filming; if output isn't authentically weak, pick a harder demo idea or add a second control run.
3. Treat "Strategist beats control" as a **hard go/no-go gate.**
4. Acknowledge prior art in the hook ("you've heard 'productize' before").
5. Don't oversimplify on-screen findings for this intermediate audience.

### Direct Answers
- **(a) Narrowing:** helps. It shrinks into a more specific, still-validated niche. Costs reach; favors conversion.
- **(b) "Stop Selling Hours" vs. "tool overwhelm":** stronger — visible paid-content trail vs. a mood usually solved by free listicles.
- **(c) Control-beat risk:** the plain-prompt output isn't bad enough, turning the proof into a strawman.
